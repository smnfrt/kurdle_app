3. App functions and target audience

Leyar: Listika Peyvan is a Kurdish/Kurmanci word game for Kurdish speakers, learners, and word-game players. It provides a Wordle-style daily challenge, a Scrabble-like word battle mode, multiplayer/friend play, progress and achievements, and a Ferheng dictionary with Kurdish word meanings. The app helps users practice Kurdish vocabulary, discover words, and play language-based games in a modern mobile experience.

4. Instructions for accessing the app's main features

No paid subscription or purchase is required to use the app. The reviewer can open the app and continue as a guest to access the core gameplay. Optional account features are available through Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email registration.

Suggested review flow:
- Launch the app.
- Continue as guest or sign in with Apple.
- Tap the main play button and start a word game.
- Try Daily Challenge for the Wordle-style mode.
- Open Ferheng to search and inspect dictionary entries.
- Open multiplayer/friend play to see invite/friend-based game flows.
- In a multiplayer match, open chat to see message, report, and block controls.
- Open profile/settings/privacy sections to review account and data options.

If a signed-in test account is needed, please use Apple Sign-In on the review device. The app also supports guest access and does not require preloaded sample files.

5. External services, tools, and platforms used
