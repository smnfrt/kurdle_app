3. App functions and target audience
Leyar: Listika Peyvan is a Kurdish/Kurmanci word game for Kurdish speakers, Kurdish learners, and word-game players. It includes a Wordle-style daily challenge, Scrabble-like word battle gameplay, multiplayer/friend play, progress/achievements, and a Ferheng dictionary. It helps users practice Kurdish vocabulary, discover words, and play language-based games.

4. Access instructions
No purchase, subscription, sample file, or special setup is required. The reviewer can launch the app and continue as guest to access core gameplay. Optional account features are available through Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email registration. Suggested flow: launch app, continue as guest or sign in with Apple, start a game, try Daily Challenge, open Ferheng, open multiplayer/friend play, open chat moderation controls, and review profile/privacy/account options. Account deletion is available in Settings under Account & Profile.

5. External services used
Leyar uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Apple Sign-In, and Google Sign-In. Firestore supports profiles, progress, multiplayer rooms, chat, reports, blocks, leaderboards, and tournament data.

6. Regional differences
There are no regional differences. Leyar functions consistently across all available regions.

7. Regulated industry / third-party material
Leyar does not operate in a regulated industry and does not provide medical, financial, gambling, or legal services. It does not include protected third-party media or third-party copyrighted gameplay content.

Additional moderation/privacy note
Multiplayer chat is user-generated content. Users can report chat messages and block players. Blocking prevents further chat and matchmaking with that player where applicable. Account/data deletion can be completed in-app from Settings > Account & Profile > Delete Account; users may also contact smnfrt@gmail.com for support.
